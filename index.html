💪 Pitcher Protocol on Rest Days & Arm Recovery Routine

Daily Rest-Day Throwing Plan

  • Day 0 (Game Day): Normal game workload + post-game arm care (bands + light flush).
  • Day 1 (Rest Day): Bands & mobility routine + 60–75 ft light toss (NO mound work).
  • Day 2 (Light Toss & Recovery): Throw 45–75 ft, Jaeger band circuit, wrist weight drills.
  • Day 3 (Touch-and-Feel): Throw 75–100 ft, optional short touch-and-feel pen (15–20 pitches).
  • Day 4 (Pre-Game Prep): Light toss only (45–60 ft), mental game review.

Arm Care & Mobility Drills

  • Wrist Weights (5 reps/set): Arm swings, 2-arm throw, Cuban press, pivot picks.
  • Jaeger Bands (10 reps/set): External rotations, forward shoulder raises, reverse flys.
  • Plyometrics: Reverse throws (2x10), pivot picks (2x10).
  • Post-Outing Rule: If pitch count exceeded 60 pitches, reduce lower-body lifting by 50%.
