⭐ Program Core Values
  • Effort: Give 100% in preparation, practice, and games. Hustle is non-negotiable.
  • Discipline: Stay committed to routines, respect team rules, and maintain self-control.
  • Accountability: Take ownership of performance and behavior. Be reliable for your teammates.
  • Growth: Strive for daily improvement, embrace challenges, and learn from success and failure.
  • Respect: Treat coaches, teammates, officials, opponents, and the game with honor.
📑 2027 Spring Training Operations (May 29 – May 31, 2027)

Spring Training provides a progressive evaluation model to establish final 2028 team rosters based on talent, performance, and consistency.

Weekend 1: Baseline Grading

Station-based individual drill evaluations (infield/outfield, arm accuracy, machine BP) and controlled scrimmages.

Weekend 2: Game Evaluation

Preliminary 3-roster game play (2 games/team). Focus on game instincts, pitch count adherence, and execution.

Weekend 3: Final Rosters

Final confirmation of roster decisions across 2028 Pierce, 2028 Borden, and 2028 Taylor squads.

🤝 Parent Expectations
  • Support the team with a positive attitude regardless of game outcome.
  • Allow coaches to coach, players to play, and umpires to officiate (no coaching from stands).
  • Communicate constructively with coaches outside game windows.
  • Ensure players arrive on time in proper uniform.
⚖️ Progressive Discipline Procedures
  1. Verbal Warning: Coach addresses issue directly.
  2. Loss of Playing Time: Player sits part of practice/game.
  3. Parent/Player Conference: Structural review meeting.
  4. Suspension / Removal: Enforced for repeated or severe violations.
