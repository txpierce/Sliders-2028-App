👨‍👩‍👧 Parents & Culture

What You Can Expect

The Sliders operate with a clear structure to ensure every athlete has the support they need. Our leadership team is made up of:

  • Head Coaches – The heartbeat of each team, responsible for practices, game execution, and culture.
  • Graduation Class Coordinator (Matt Pierce) – Oversee all teams, ensuring consistency, alignment, and long-term development.
  • Social Media, Finance, and Recruiting Coordinators – Specialists who promote our athletes, ensure financial transparency, and guide families through the college recruiting process.

Our Commitment to You

  • Player Development First: Practices and games are designed with situational focus, growth, and accountability.
  • Safety & Compliance: We strictly follow SafeSport compliance, Pitch Smart guidelines and monitor recovery and player usage.
  • Clear Communication: Weekly TWIB (This Week in Baseball) calendars will keep you informed of schedules, practices, and travel details.
  • Culture & Discipline: Our teams represent more than baseball. Respect, effort, and integrity are non-negotiables.

How You Can Get Involved

We encourage parent participation through volunteer opportunities such as dugout support, travel coordination, fundraising, and media assistance. Each role helps strengthen our community and allows us to focus on the athletes.

Looking Ahead

As we begin this season, know that your child is joining not just a team, but a program built on trust, accountability, and shared purpose. We are grateful for the commitment your family has made and look forward to walking this journey together.

If you have questions at any point, please reach out to your Head Coach or Regional Director. You will also receive regular communication from your team staff regarding schedules, travel, and events.

Thank you for trusting us with your athlete’s development. Let’s make this season a memorable one.

With gratitude and excitement,

President – Sliders Baseball
